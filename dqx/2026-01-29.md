# Back because of Ruberangis

## Back Again Because of Ruberangis

I started logging in again.

Not too long ago, they announced **Level 3 Ruberangis**, which means Level 3 Abyss Criminals are not only officially confirmed, but Ruberangis is coming, and soon we’ll get Level 3s of the others too.

The Abyss Criminals are, straight up, my favorite fights in the game. Honestly, they’re some of my favorite content in DQX period. So hearing that we’re getting harder versions of them made me immediately want to be there day one and learn and master these fights at their absolute hardest.

Whether I’ll be able to is another question. I don’t speak Japanese well enough, and I don’t have a lot of help. Day one might be unrealistic. Maybe day two or three. We’ll see. I’m still really excited.

This announcement is what pulled me out of my DQX break. I genuinely don’t think there’s any other news they could’ve announced that would’ve excited me more. This is it.

---

## Money, Kids Time, and Reality

So I started logging in every day again.

These superbosses are expensive. If I want to fight them, I need money. Luckily, I have about a one-month head start, and more importantly, certainty that these bosses are coming.

For now, the plan is to log in every day at **11 PM**, because that’s when Kids Time starts.

At 11 PM, I can go do Demon Cell farming (魔因細胞, I think? I honestly don’t really know), and hopefully make around **1 million gold a day**. If that works out, I should have about **45 million gold** by the time the new boss drops.

That’s not a lot, per se, but it should at least be enough to afford the equipment needed to fight these bosses. Prices always go up at release, and I have no idea what gear will actually be required, so I can’t prepare ahead of time.

Being broke is not good lol.

Let’s hope this works.

---

## Logging In Means Feelings, Unfortunately

Since I started logging in regularly, things happened. As usual.

I have two friends, **Hide** and **Nagi**, and I’m chill with both of them. It seems like both of them have returned from their own breaks too. Maybe Ruberangis-related, maybe not. I wouldn’t know, but I assume so.

I care about them. I love them. I’m pretty sure Hide loves me.

And yet, I feel anxious and uneasy.

People in this game have been cruel to me in the past. Even when I try to put up walls to protect myself, I’m terrible at enforcing them. If I were smart, I’d stop putting myself into these situations over and over like a dumbass.

But I am who I am, I guess.

That anxiety got worse when I looked at Nagi’s friends list on stream and saw that cursed name again.

もも.

The person who gave me the most trauma in this game. The person who broke promises over and over, blamed it on me, openly discriminated against me, and never apologized. The person who taught me that, in this game, people see you as nothing more than a monkey who can press buttons if you’re not Japanese.

And he was on Nagi’s friends list.

I was just starting to feel comfortable again. Guess not.

It made me feel like I have no allies, no real connections, and no one to hold me together in this game. Dan isn’t here either. He has medical school, and even if he were, dumping all my emotions on him wouldn’t be fair. That would make me a shitty friend, and I try not to be.

I still have people like Tony, Andy, and Ghost. They’re solid.

But in this game?

I don’t really know who I have.

---

## A Genuinely Good Surprise

Enough doom for a moment.

Something really good happened.

I was on Twitter and came across someone whose DQX characters I’ve always thought were really cute. I love their Weddie. I love their Ogre. I see like four different names associated with them: ぬこ, みぽぬこ, るぃ… I honestly don’t know which one is correct. Their handle is **@donnneko_4z**, so I’ll go with that.

It turns out they’re a mother with children my age, which definitely made me feel young as hell. I imagine the opposite is probably true too.

They were tweeting about a Valentine’s chocolate event. I didn’t really read it. I just liked it because their character was cute. When I replied, they reached out and explained the event to me, how it worked, how to dress, and how to participate.

I already kind of knew, but the passion they had when explaining it made me happy. It felt nice having someone not be scared to talk to me. That’s not something I’m used to, so I never know how to respond.

At past events, I’m used to people avoiding me, deliberately not talking to me, or having me blocked on Twitter while wandering around anyway. I usually don’t even get invited.

I still remember the **Divali** event being run without anyone telling me.  
The only actual Indian player in DQX.

It happens, I guess.

So I was cautious. I told her that I only go to events if I have someone with me. The only times I’ve ever felt comfortable were when I went with someone like Konoha back in the day, or when Sugar took me. Events are hard, and not everyone wants to baby someone who doesn’t speak Japanese.

And she said:

**“Let’s go together.”**

That genuinely caught me off guard.

She was willing to help me and stay with me during something that causes me extreme anxiety. We talked more, planned things out, made matching outfits, and somehow the world now knows we’re going to this event as a pair.

I had no idea that would happen.

But I’m really happy.

DQX moms are always the sweetest people I meet. Every single time.

She also invited me to a few other events coming up before the big one. Demon-themed stuff, others too. I said yes. I’m excited. You’ll probably see diaries for those days as well, assuming I survive coding the galleries.

## Trying to Make Something Work

I’m still a member of **Shion’s room**.

There are still people there who scare me. I don’t feel guaranteed safety. A lot of things still make me uneasy. But right now, this feels like something I might be okay sticking with.

I’ve had long, nuanced conversations with both **Shion** and **Ekari**.

Ekari still scares me a bit. She reminds me of my sister. But I don’t think she’s a bad person. She genuinely tries to understand me, and that’s not something I can take for granted.

We talked about Japanese culture, DQX room balance, and behaviors that help or hurt the group. Instead of getting upset, she talked to me directly. I do wish she had more empathy for how hard this all feels for me. It honestly feels like I’m playing on impossible difficulty.

But it’s still leeway. And I rarely get that.

My conversation with Shion surprised me too. He’s quiet, which makes me nervous, but when I brought things up, we had a long and honest talk.

He told me that I’m in a difficult situation he doesn’t fully understand, but that he doesn’t want me to restrain or erase my individuality.

Hearing that from someone in a collectivist society genuinely shocked me. I’m not used to people telling me I don’t need to disappear to belong.

So yeah, I’m not comfortable around everyone here. But for once, I want to try to make this work.

These are the kinds of people I’d wake up at 5 AM for to do ADF if they lost a member. I already have, multiple times.

---

## For Now

I’m still playing with the French players too. They’re welcoming. They talk openly. They post art I actually care about. I like it there.

DQX is exhausting. The urge to quit comes back a lot. But for at least a month, I’ll keep trying.

There’s still a lot I’m afraid of. People like もも. Player events. Even Shion’s room sometimes. Everything feels like it could fall apart at any moment.

And Demon Cell farming is honestly not fun at all.

But I’ll try.

One step in front of the other, for as long as I can.

There are still good people.  
ぴーやん has always treated me well.  
めるくん too, even if he scares me.

I’m just going to keep going.
