<h1>A Really Good Stretch in DQX</h1>

<p>
The Japanese demon photoshoot was <em>so</em> much fun. I went with Nuko, and I’m honestly super appreciative that I was able to go with her at all. We took tons of pictures, and I’m still really happy I even managed to find out about the event in the first place.
</p>

<p>
I took photos with so many people, and I loved seeing how everyone approached it. A lot of people used purple skin, but what really stood out to me was how everyone paired dark skin with super saturated, vibrant colours. It looked genuinely amazing. The whole event was just really joyful, and I loved it.
</p>

<p>
In Shion’s room, things have also been feeling better. I’ve been talking with Shion more, and it hasn’t been stressful. There are still some people there who make me anxious, but overall it’s slowly becoming more comfortable, which I really appreciate.
</p>

<p>
This time around, I spent a lot of time with ツムジ. I’m studying Japanese, and he helped me a few times, and we talked a bit. He’s around my age, also a male Weddie, and he plays a lot of different games on Steam, so it felt easy to connect.
</p>

<p>
I also ran into Nyanmaru while he was doing his daily Abyss Criminal Noxelia fight. He wasn’t streaming, so I kind of seized the opportunity and asked if he wanted to do the fight together, and if he’d be okay with VC. He asked if Japanese was okay, and I was like… duh, lol.
</p>

<p>
We only talked for maybe ten minutes, but it was enough to get a good sense of who he is, and I felt comfortable talking to him. He’s also a male demon Weddie, he loves animals, and we talked about DQX, the problems I’ve been having in the game, and people he plays with like Hide and Haru. It was really nice actually meeting and talking with him, and I should probably start dropping by his streams more.
</p>

<p>
Tatsuya also came back to DQX for a bit. I found out that a lot of people don’t really like him, which surprised me. I don’t speak Japanese well enough to understand the reasons, but to me he just seemed like a normal guy, and I enjoyed spending time with him.
</p>

<hr>

<p>
Then there’s ぴーやん’s group of friends. There are some people there, like Mell, who honestly scare me. And then there was マーフィー. I’d seen her around a lot, but she was always super quiet in VC, and when I tried messaging her before she didn’t reply. I convinced myself she hated me, which gave me a lot of anxiety.
</p>
<div class="gallery">
  <img src="/assets/images/dqx/2026-02/Screenshot 2026-02-02 111445.png" alt="Winning with Mell and Mafi">
</div>
<p>
But we took a picture together, I sent it to her, and we ended up having a really nice conversation. She explained some things, and she also followed me on Twitter, which genuinely surprised me. That’s what led to us talking more.
</p>


<p>
It turns out she’s just really hyperactive but also doesn’t talk much. She listened to me while I explained a lot about my anxiety, why certain things affect me the way they do, and why Mell scares me. Then I found out she’s Mell’s girlfriend, which actually lined up with what Piyan had mentioned before, that Mell found a really nice girlfriend. And yeah, that checks out.
</p>

<p>
Because of that, I feel way more comfortable with this group now. I’m definitely going to start coming around more often. It feels like a nice place to be.
</p>

<p>
I also did the Valentine’s event with Heltaall, which was really fun. She has such a friendly, welcoming personality and feels very familiar to be around. We did silly stuff, took pictures, and messed around with outfits. She’s always super expressive with her emotes in-game, and honestly just a really sweet friend.
</p>

<p>
As for the Valentine’s event itself, I still don’t feel especially drawn to most of the female characters in DQX. A lot of them feel kind of skin-deep to me. Estella is still an exception. But this year did have some good moments.
</p>

<p>
Marone is our in-game mother, and while I don’t think she’s an especially strong character, she does tug at the heartstrings. Lechtalis, though, I genuinely love. She’s just really lovely.
</p>

<p>
Lynn being back this year was also great. She’s really well written, and the Fairy Library is still one of my favourite parts of DQX. I’m glad she’s here again. I don’t know who I’ll vote for, and honestly, I’m not sure I even care that much. I got the glam, and that’s cool. I’m mostly looking forward to White Day, since the Valentine’s and White Day events usually have great outfits.
</p>

<hr>

<p>
I also spent some quiet time with ルリ. She’s always been lovely to me, and her husband ルカ has always treated me well too. I’m really glad to be close with them. Even if we don’t do much together, I like just being with her. We sat together and enjoyed each other’s company, and that meant a lot to me.
</p>

</p>

<p>
And on top of all that, Enya invited me to their DQX room. That means I’ll be able to go to more player events and do more things together. They don’t use Discord, but I already know Enya, so I’m hopeful. I’ll do my best.
